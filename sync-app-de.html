Dunable Guitars

Airtable → Shopify DE Sync

Push checked records from Airtable to dunable-de.com

How it works:
1. In Airtable, check the "Sync to Shopify DE" box on any DE model variant you want to push.
2. Click Sync below — products are created as drafts in Shopify and added to their model collection.
3. Already-synced products (matched by UPC) are skipped automatically.
4. Go to Shopify admin to review and publish when ready.
